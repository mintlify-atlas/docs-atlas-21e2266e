SkillTanki is a browser-based tank arcade built in the spirit of the 2010 classic. Open the page, pick a call sign and a tank, and you're in a battle within seconds — no account, no downloads, no shop. Everything is unlocked from the start: 7 hulls, 9 turrets, 101 paints, and 91 maps across five battle modes. Play solo against smart bots, invite friends to a private room, or jump into an open public match.
