SkillTanki gives you complete freedom to assemble your tank before every battle. There are no unlocks, no currencies, and no pay gates — everything is available from the first second you open the game.

## How loadouts work

A tank is built from three independent choices made in the **Garage**:

| Slot | What it changes | Default |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **Hull** | Speed, health pool, physical size, and handling. The hull is the chassis — it determines how you move and how much punishment you can take. | Hunter |
| **Turret** | Weapon type, fire rate, range, and damage style. The turret sits on top of the hull and rotates independently. | Smoky |
| **Paint** | Visual appearance only. 101 paints are available from the start; none affect stats. | Team colour |

The defaults — **Hunter + Smoky** — are intentionally balanced. They are a reliable starting point, but no combination is strictly "best". The right loadout depends on the map, the mode, and your play style.

<Note>
  Your selected hull, turret, and paint are saved locally. They carry over into every new battle (solo or multiplayer) until you change them in the Garage.
</Note>

## Hulls

Each hull has four visual and statistical upgrade tiers — **M0** through **M3**. The tiers are cosmetic and stat upgrades (higher tiers look more elaborate and have improved stats) but do not change the fundamental character of the hull. All seven hulls are available at all tiers immediately.

| Hull | Role | Playstyle notes |
|------|------|----------------|
| **Wasp** | Fast glass-cannon | The lightest and quickest hull. Extremely high top speed and acceleration, very low health. A Wasp gets anywhere first but dies in a handful of hits. Best with hit-and-run weapons like Railgun or Twins. |
| **Hornet** | Agile skirmisher | Slightly heavier than the Wasp but still fast. Trades a little speed for meaningfully more survivability. A common competitive choice because it blends mobility with just enough health to survive a mistake. |
| **Hunter** | Balanced all-rounder *(default)* | The middle of every stat. Neither the fastest nor the toughest, the Hunter is forgiving for new players and effective enough for veterans on most maps. Pairs well with any turret. |
| **Viking** | Durable pusher | Above-average health and moderate speed. The Viking can absorb more punishment than the Hunter without becoming sluggish. Strong in Team Deathmatch where outlasting the enemy matters. |
| **Dictator** | Heavy brawler | High health, notable weight — useful for ramming and holding chokepoints. Speed is below average but not prohibitive. Favoured with short-range high-damage turrets like Firebird or Twins. |
| **Titan** | Armoured heavyweight | One of the two heaviest hulls. Very high health, slow movement. Excels at holding a position or escorting a flag carrier in CTF. Pairs naturally with Thunder (area denial) or Shaft (long-range attrition). |
| **Mammoth** | Slow fortress | The largest and most durable hull in the game. Exceptional health pool, very low speed. A Mammoth in the right position is extremely hard to destroy but cannot reposition quickly. Favoured by experienced players who know the map well enough to pre-position. |

<Info>
  Hull modifications (M0–M3) are selected in the Garage alongside hull choice. A higher modification looks different and has better stats, but the hull's fundamental speed-to-health character stays the same across all four tiers.
</Info>

## Turrets

Nine turrets, each a different philosophy for landing damage. Pick the one whose mechanics suit how you want to play.

<CardGroup cols={3}>

<Card title="Smoky" icon="crosshairs">
  **Type:** Instant-hit cannon

  The default turret and the one most players start with. Fires a single projectile with zero travel time — aim at the target and it hits immediately. Reliable damage, moderate fire rate, manageable spread. A genuine all-rounder that works on every hull at every range. If you are unsure which turret to use, Smoky is never wrong.
</Card>

<Card title="Twins" icon="bolt">
  **Type:** Rapid twin-barrel cannon

  Two barrels alternate to produce a very high rate of fire. Individual shots deal less damage than Smoky, but the sustained DPS at close-to-medium range exceeds most other turrets. Particularly devastating when paired with a fast hull (Wasp, Hornet) that can get into and stay in the kill zone. Falls off at long range due to spread accumulation.
</Card>

<Card title="Ricochet" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
  **Type:** Bouncing shell cannon

  Shells ricochet off walls and floors before hitting a target — or dissipating after a bounce limit. The indirect fire path punishes tanks hiding behind cover and absolutely devastates opponents in tight corridors, enclosed rooms, and tunnels where bounces multiply damage. High mechanical ceiling: mastering the geometry of a map unlocks angles impossible for straight-fire weapons.
</Card>

<Card title="Railgun" icon="zap">
  **Type:** Piercing beam (instant-hit)

  Fires an instant, thin beam that passes through multiple tanks in a line — every tank the beam crosses takes full damage. Strong individual hit damage and long effective range. Excellent on open maps with sightlines. Even under Double Damage the Railgun will not one-shot a medium hull (Hunter); the supply multiplier is intentionally capped at ×1.5 to preserve balance.
</Card>

<Card title="Thunder" icon="cloud-bolt">
  **Type:** Splash-damage cannon

  Shells detonate on impact with a damaging radius. Any tank caught in the blast — not just the direct hit target — takes damage. Superb for area denial at chokepoints, flag bases, and crowded corridors. Splash damage rewards positioning and map knowledge; poor for precise duels at long range. Works beautifully with heavy hulls (Titan, Mammoth) that can stand still and control a zone.
</Card>

<Card title="Shaft" icon="scope">
  **Type:** Charged sniper cannon

  Two firing modes in one turret. **Tap fire** delivers a standard shot at close to medium range — fast, no charge required. **Hold fire** begins a visible charge animation with a targeting laser visible to both shooter and target; releasing a full charge dramatically increases damage and accuracy at long range. Bots using Shaft tap up close and charge at distance — the same is true for skilled human players. The highest skill ceiling of any straight-fire turret.
</Card>

<Card title="Firebird" icon="fire">
  **Type:** Flamethrower jet (continuous)

  Projects a cone of flame that deals continuous damage to any tank inside it. Held fire keeps burning; the moment the target leaves the cone the damage stops. No reload as long as the energy gauge holds — the jet drains the gauge and it regenerates when not firing. Lethal in narrow passages where a target cannot escape the cone. Weak at range; requires closing the distance aggressively. Pairs naturally with fast hulls or heavy hulls that can pin a target.
</Card>

<Card title="Freeze" icon="snowflake">
  **Type:** Cryo jet (continuous)

  Works like Firebird mechanically (energy gauge, held cone), but deals reduced direct damage in exchange for **chilling** the target: a frozen tank loses speed and acceleration for as long as it stays in the beam. The slow effect makes it effortless to keep the cone on target, letting a teammate finish the kill. A natural support weapon in team modes — Freeze a flag carrier and your team can catch them easily.
</Card>

<Card title="Isida" icon="heart-pulse">
  **Type:** Beam — drain enemies, heal allies

  The only turret with two opposing effects controlled by who you aim at. Aimed at an **enemy**: drains their health and deals damage. Aimed at a **teammate**: restores their health. The Isida is SkillTanki's dedicated support weapon. Bot Isida users prioritise healing a wounded teammate over attacking an enemy — the same reasoning applies to human players in team modes. Invaluable for escorting a CTF flag carrier or keeping a Mammoth alive in a siege. Requires close proximity; the beam has a finite reach.
</Card>

</CardGroup>

## Paints

**101 paints** are available from the first launch — none locked behind progress or payments. Paints are purely cosmetic: they change the colour and pattern on your hull and turret but do not alter any stat. In team modes, if you leave your paint as the default (no paint selected), your tank takes on the Red or Blue team colour automatically so allies and enemies are always visually distinct.

<Tip>
  In team modes, setting a bright or high-contrast paint helps teammates spot you quickly in a crowd — especially useful if you are playing Isida and want allies to stay in your beam range.
</Tip>
